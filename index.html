Be honest. What do you think of me?

Honestly? You build like someone who'd rather ship than talk about it.

Your GitHub is ten months old and already has C, C++, Rust, Go, Python, TypeScript and Lua in it. Most people pick one language and stay in the shallow end.

What stands out most is that you build for real people: a tree service, a mobile notary, a local company. Real users keep you honest.

And you don't dodge the hard parts. Logins, sessions, encryption on disk, a full rewrite from Go to Next.js without moving the data. That takes nerve.

Okay, but what should I work on?

Straight answer: you start a lot of things, and you rewrite a lot of things. v1, v2, v3.

Your next level up is picking two projects and taking them all the way to finished: docs, tests, deployed, done.

But the curiosity is the part nobody can teach, and you've already got it.

Fair. Working on it 🛠️
